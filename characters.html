My Favorite Marvel Characters

Deadpool

Deadpool - also known as Wade Wilson - is a Marvel character with superhuman healing abilities.
He was originally introduced as a villain but later on became more of an antihero. He is a strong assassin who uses guns and swords who became popular for his unique fighting style and his sarcastic humor.

View Powers
  • Superhuman strength and durability
  • Regenerative healing
  • Fourth wall breaks
  • Skilled swordsman, martial artist, and master assassin

Gwenpool

Gwenpool - also known as Gwendolyn Poole - is a Marvel character who comes from the real world (our universe) and is transported into the Marvel Universe.
Unlike most other superheroes, she is aware that she is inside a comic book and often breaks the fourth wall.

View Powers
  • Outside knowledge of the Marvel Universe
  • Interdimensional travel
  • Fourth wall breaks
  • Reality manipulation
  • Hand-to-hand combat
