Welcome to the Marvel Universe.

Marvel is one of the biggest comic book franchises in the world. This webpage is a personal blog that will be written from the perspective of a Marvel fan. We will explore some of my favorite characters, the game Marvel Rivals, and some personal comic book recommendations.

I have been a Marvel fan for a long time and have collected many comics throughout the years. Even though I also have watched all the movies and TV shows, this webpage will focus more on other types of media that are not very mainstream as the MCU (Marvel Cinematic Universe).

This is a student project created for educational purposes. Marvel and its characters are property of Marvel Entertainment.
