Purpose

Marvel Rivals is a team-based multiplayer game that brings popular Marvel heroes and villains into competitive battles. Players choose characters with unique abilities and work together to defeat the opposing team.

The purpose of Marvel Rivals is to encourage a strategy while using each characters strengths. Different roles such as Duelists, Vanguards, and Strategists require players to balance offense, defense, and support. Success depends on teamwork, communication, and smart use of abilities.

Roles

Role Description Example Characters
Duelist High damage characters focused on eliminating opponents. Iron Man, Scarlet Witch, Phoenix, Psylocke
Vanguard Tank characters who absorb damage and protect teammates. Hulk, Groot, Captain America, Thor
Strategist Support characters who provide healing or utility abilities. Loki, Invisible Woman, Gambit

My Thoughts

I started playing this game ever since it came out in late 2024 and have not stopped yet. It is a fun game with new characters being released every month. It is for sure one of my favorite and most played games and I could loose myself in it for hours.
